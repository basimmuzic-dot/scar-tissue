# SCAR TISSUE — Camera Walkthroughs for Concept Art

*A shot-by-shot guide to what the player's first-person camera sees in each space. Use it to brief concept artists, environment artists, lighting artists and the cinematics team. Each shot lists framing, light, palette, sound and the one detail that must read.*

**Global camera rules**
- First-person, eye height ~1.6 m, slight natural sway. Hands and forearms visible at bottom of frame when raised.
- Lenses: ~70° vertical FOV by default; narrow to ~55° for intimate scenes, widen for cathedral spaces.
- Light is motivated by real sources (skylights, bulbs, fire, glow). No unmotivated fill. Blacks stay black.
- Film grain light, slight lens fogging on cold/wet spaces, no heavy post-processing blur.

## 1. ADMISSIONS

**1.1 Entrance Hall (establishing).** Wide, slightly low angle. Black-and-white chequered floor buckled and wet. A shaft of grey daylight falls through the broken ceiling onto a toppled wheelchair and a *neat pair of shoes*. Chandelier hangs from one chain. Palette: bone white, cool grey, a stain of rust-brown at the knee-high tide-line. *Must read: someone was here a moment ago.*

**1.2 The Staircase.** Looking up the grand stair. Treads furred with green, the upper flight dissolving into black. A faint **hand-print in the dust** on the banister. *Must read: the building goes up and the dark goes with it.*

**1.3 Reception.** Medium shot across the desk. Cracked glass partition, brass bell, dead desk-lamp, the ledger *open*. Dust everywhere except a clean patch where a hand rested. Palette: warm brass against grey-green. *Must read: a clean place in a dusty room.*

**1.4 Records Room.** Down an aisle of steel cabinets, drawers hanging open. One aisle is neat. Narrow footprints lead in, none out. Green banker's lamp on its side casts a sickly pool. *Must read: the tidy aisle is the aisle that matters.*

**1.5 Staff Corridor.** Long corridor, mustard linoleum, a pail catching drips, a fire door at the far end with green wire-glass. Vanishing point is a green glow. *Must read: depth and a destination.*

**1.6 The Silence Beat.** Static shot of the corridor. Lighting does not change. Only sound: the drip stops. Hold for 8 seconds. *Must read: nothing happens, and that is the scare.*

**1.7 Stairwell.** Looking down a spiral of iron and stone. Blue-white light rising from below, wet handrail glinting. *Must read: descent, water, cold glow.*

## 2. THE BATHS

**2.1 Stair Landing.** Amber emergency lamps, sick and warm. Water-stained tile, a cold draught visible as mist. A row of green lockers recedes into dark.

**2.2 Locker Hall.** Slight Dutch tilt for unease. Locker doors hang at different angles. **Small bare wet footprints** cross the floor into shadow. A rail of rubber caps hangs like skins.

**2.3 Baths A (the money shot).** Very wide, centred, symmetrical. A cathedral of pale-blue tile, a vaulted glass ceiling, shafts of dusty light falling onto **circular copper-rimmed baths** with perfectly flat water. Reflections double the hall. *Must read: beautiful, vast, too still.*

**2.4 The Treatment Frames.** Close, low angle. Tall iron rings with leather straps, clean and gleaming. Water pools at their feet. *Must read: they are made for a body.*

**2.5 The Pump Room.** Tight, claustrophobic, handheld sway. Rust-orange light through grating. Water rushing visible **under the floor**. The one tidy cabinet gleams.

**2.6 Catwalk (from above).** High angle looking down. The baths below form a ring of dark circles like eyes. The gantry stretches ahead, a gap breaking it at a distance. *Must read: height, rot, and a gap.*

**2.7 The Figure Across the Water.** Long lens, narrow FOV, shallow depth. A pale, slightly bent figure stands far across the flooded floor, its reflection beneath it a moment late. *Must read: it is standing the way the player stands.*

**2.8 Baths B / Deep Pool.** Black glassy surface. Pale tile glimmers far down. The ledge hugs the wall. The lift glows faintly on the far side. Palette: ink black and sick yellow.

**2.9 The Lift Mirror.** Brass, warm, intimate. Camera faces the clouded mirror. Player's reflection centre-frame; behind them, **soft and out of focus, a second figure**.

## 3. THE SURGICAL WING

**3.1 The Corridor.** Teal tile, white ceiling, caged lights. A row of gurneys with perfectly smooth sheets. Green exit sign at the end. Sterile palette; the only warmth is the player's flashlight.

**3.2 Operating Theatre.** From the door: the round amphitheatre dropping to a central table under a vast overhead lamp. Tiers of wooden seats, a glass gallery above. Instruments laid out. *A single chair beside the table.* Warm lamp light against cold teal.

**3.3 The Gallery.** From above, looking down through glass. The lamp's disc reflects on the glass with the player's reflection standing inside it, tiny. *Must read: you are being looked at, even when alone.*

**3.4 Instrument Store.** Brown glass bottles, Latin labels, tools hung like trophies. A cabinet of jars: pale threads in clear fluid, each labelled with a name.

**3.5 Henn's Office.** Green leather, dark panelling. A huge **nervous-system diagram** on the wall, with hand-drawn lines linking *people* instead of organs. A reel-to-reel on the desk.

---

## 4. THE MIRROR WARD

**4.1 The Doorway.** Long, slightly low, symmetrical down the ward. Forty beds, tall mirrors between them, the lit window at the far end repeating infinitely. *The widest, most beautiful frame in the game.*

**4.2 The Drawings.** Close, soft focus. Crayon hands in chains. One drawing: a tall dark-haired figure in a white coat. *MARA WILL COME.*

**4.3 The Mirrors.** Medium, tracking. The player's reflection arrives late. Further on, reflections carry wounds the player doesn't have.

**4.4 The Graft Beds.** Wide, slightly high. Heavy beds, brass ports, a **square cut in the floor** with warm light rising like breath. Brass plaque in foreground.

**4.5 Nell's Bed.** Intimate, narrow FOV. A patchwork quilt, a row of small objects on the windowsill, the plaster hand-cast catching the window light. *The only warm, soft frame in the ward.*

**4.6 The Understudy in the Doorway.** Back-lit by grey window light, silhouetted, hand raised, palm open. It carries every wound the player does. *Must read: not a threat, an offer.*

---

## 5. THE BOILER HOUSE

**5.1 Furnace Hall (establishing).** Very wide, low. Three immense riveted boilers, forty feet of blackened brick above. Soot thick on every surface. A single firebox glows. Palette: black, rust, ember orange. Heat shimmer.

**5.2 The Soot Floor.** Overhead close shot. Footprints and handprints in soft black soot, the player's among older ones. *Must read: the player is leaving a trail others have left.*

**5.3 The Coal Store.** Dunes of black, a frozen conveyor, a wheelbarrow of coal. A child's mitten, a rosary, spectacles half-buried.

**5.4 Tomas's Room.** Small, warm, tidy. Paraffin stove light, a cot, a radio. **Walls covered in dense pencil tally marks**, floor to ceiling. A staff photograph with every face intact.

**5.5 The Collapse.** Wide to extreme close. Rotten beams, a flooded pit, the floor dropping away with a long groan.

---

## 6. THE FOUNDATIONS

**6.1 The Stone Stair.** Rough hewn stone, wet, descending into black water. The first pale **cord** appears on the wall, thin as hair.

**6.2 The Vaults.** Low, rounded chambers, knee-deep black water. Cords pulse faintly violet in time with the player's heartbeat. Ripples in the water *answer* the player's steps.

**6.3 The Wall of Faces.** Slow tracking shot. Niches along the wall hold soft, pale faces formed from cord and tissue. Eyes closed, lips moving together. *Not decaying. Resting.*

**6.4 The Chorus Room (reveal).** The biggest shot in the game. Slow, wide, a gentle rise as the player enters. A vast dome, mist at the top, walls lined with pale living tissue, a broad black pool, a single stone platform at its centre. Violet and pale pink-grey light. *Awe before fear.*

**6.5 Nell.** Medium, low angle from the water. A young woman asleep in a too-old gown, hundreds of fine cords rising from her body like hair, streaming into the walls. When she breathes, the whole room brightens and dims.

**6.6 The Clock in the Water.** Looking up. Through the mist overhead, impossibly, the face of the clock tower, stopped at **2:47**, as if seen through water.
