# SCAR TISSUE — Opening Cinematic & Title Sequence

*Runtime ~3 min 30 s. Plays before Ward 1, then hands control to the player at the gate. Almost no dialogue. It establishes Mara, Nell, the postcard, the building, and the central image of the game: a hand.*

---

## Part A — Cold Open: 1979 (≈50 s)

**Black.** A child's breathing, quick and shallow. Then a small voice, close to the microphone:

> **NELL (9, whispering):** *"It's okay. It hurts a bit. It's okay."*

**Cut to:** a bright, sunlit kitchen. Warm, yellow, slightly overexposed, like an old home movie. A pen scratches on paper. At the table sits **MARA, 17**, in a school cardigan, signing a stack of forms with a tired, careful hand. Across from her, a **clinic administrator** (face never shown, only a pressed suit and a clipboard) slides the next page forward.

**Insert:** the signature, *M. Voss*, in clean looping letters, repeated page after page.

**Cut to:** a staircase in the same house. **NELL, 9**, sits on the third step in pyjamas, clutching the banister, watching her sister sign. Her left hand is wrapped in a bandage. Mara looks up and sees her. She smiles, a worn, brave smile, and **draws a small circle on the back of Nell's good hand with the pen**, a habit between them.

> **MARA (17, soft):** *"It's just to help the pain. I'll visit every Sunday. I promise."*

**Hold** on the circle drawn in blue ink.

**Cut to black.** The sound of a car door closing. Rain.

---

## Part B — The Drive: 1994 (≈70 s)

**Fade in:** the same blue circle, but now a **photograph**: a small hand, a crescent scar across the knuckles, the circle faintly inked on the skin. It is held by an adult hand, steady but with a thin white scar across the thumb. The photograph is attached to a **postcard**, the back facing the camera. The address is written in elegant looping letters. **It is Mara's handwriting.**

**Pull back:** Mara, now 32, drives a battered estate car on a washed-out mountain road. Wipers beat. The postcard is propped against the dashboard. Her face is lit green by the dashboard glow; there are shadows under her eyes. A surgical pager hangs from her belt, silent.

**Insert shots (quick, no dialogue):**
- A hospital badge: **DR. M. VOSS — TRAUMA SURGERY**.
- The postmark: **ST. ALDRIC. NOV 1994.** (a place she was told had been demolished.)
- A cassette in the dash: she presses eject, takes it, and puts it away unplayed.
- A coat on the passenger seat, folded neat and a child's size, which she has carried for fifteen years and never opened.

**The road floods.** The car slows, then stalls, water to the hubs. Mara looks at the postcard once more, takes a breath, and steps out into the rain.

> **MARA (to herself, barely audible):** *"Eighty-one steps to the door. I'll count."*

---

## Part C — First Sight of the Sanatorium (≈40 s)

**Handheld, shoulder-height, following Mara from behind** as she walks up the flooded drive between dead poplars.

**Wide reveal:** the façade through the mist. A pale stone hospital, long and symmetrical. The **clock tower**, hands stopped at **2:47**. Dozens of dark windows.

**Slow push to the third floor.** One window is lit: a warm, steady amber square. A **small hand-shaped shadow** passes across it, then is gone.

**Mara stops.** She raises her own hand slightly, then lowers it.

> **MARA:** *"Nell."*

The sound of the rain thins. Underneath it, for the first time, a **soft, many-voiced hum**, so quiet it might be the wind.

---

## Part D — Title Sequence (≈50 s)

Over the dark of the open doors, the camera moves **inside**, into black. The hum swells.

A sequence of slow macro images emerges from the dark, each held for 3–4 seconds, dissolving into the next:

1. **A crayon drawing** of joined paper-doll hands, slowly receiving a drop of water.
2. **A scalpel**, held in a gloved hand, tracing a line of light across a mirror.
3. **A circle** drawn in blue ink on skin, slowly fading into a crescent scar.
4. **A copper bath-rim** catching a single skylight, ripples spreading outward from the centre.
5. **A row of mirrors** receding into dark, each reflecting a slightly different hand.
6. **A clock face** at 2:47, then the second hand, impossibly, **ticking**, once.

**Title card:** black screen, a thin white line draws itself into a **long, pale scar** across the middle of the frame. As it finishes, it parts slightly, like skin, and the letters of the title emerge from the gap:

# **SCAR TISSUE**

*Subtitle in smaller type, appearing a second later:* **a game about what we carry**

**Sound:** the hum resolves into a single clean chord, then cuts to **silence**. A hand-bell, once.

**Cut to gameplay:** the player stands at the open doors in the rain.

---

## Direction Notes
- **Palette:** Part A warm and washed yellow; Part B cold green and grey; Part C desaturated; title sequence near-monochrome with a single blue accent (the circle).
- **Music:** none for Parts A–C except a barely audible hum. A solo piano motif (four descending notes) appears only when Mara looks at the photograph, and returns in the endings.
- **No exposition.** The audience should understand *Mara signed something, Nell was hurt, and Mara is going back* without a word explaining it.
- **Recurring image:** the blue circle on the hand. It appears in the opening, in Ward 4's drawings, in the Chorus Room, and in the final epilogue.
