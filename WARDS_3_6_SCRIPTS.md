# SCAR TISSUE — Scripts for Wards 3–6 (and the Ending Scenes)

*Story beats, dialogue and found documents. Continues from `WARDS_1_2.md` and the Mirror Ward scene. Mirror Ward (Ward 4) dialogue lives in `MIRROR_WARD_SCENE.md`; this file covers its supporting documents only.*

**Voices:** MARA (controlled, dry, guilty) · TOMAS (older, tired, careful) · HENN (recorded; warm, gentle, increasingly obsessed) · NELL (present-day: calm, sometimes in two voices) · THE CHORUS (many voices, one note)

---

# WARD 3 — THE SURGICAL WING

## Arrival (lift opens)
**Visual:** The lift opens onto cold teal tile. The air is dry and chemical, faintly sweet.

**Tomas (radio, static):** *"You found the lift. Good. Mara… that is your name, isn't it? I read it in the ledger, years ago. Keep going up. Don't stay in the theatre. It likes it in there."*

**Mara:** *"It likes it? Who are you?"*

**Tomas:** *"Someone who should have stopped this thirty years ago."* (static) *"Don't leave your wounds open."*

## Corridor — found: Nursing Log, 1961
> *"Dr. H. has taken to sitting up with the patients after rounds. He says they sleep better when he's in the room. Patient 14 asked him if he could feel the others too. He said, 'A little.' I think he meant it."*

## Operating Theatre — Mara's reaction
**Mara:** *"They kept this room clean. Thirty years of water and rot, and this is still… sterile. Who's been doing this?"*
*(She looks at the chair beside the table.)*
**Mara:** *"Somebody sits here. Somebody who watches."*

## Instrument Store — The Jars
**Mara (reading labels):** *"Alder, T. Brennan, M. Okafor, I. Small Tom."* *(She stops.)* *"These aren't specimens. These are people."*

She finds the jar labelled **VOSS, N.** It is **empty**, but its fluid is faintly warm.

## The Gallery — The Understudy Close Up (first time with glass between them)
The Understudy stands in the theatre below, in the lamplight, facing the table. Mara, above, behind glass. It raises its hand. She is holding her own hand up without knowing why. The glass between them is the only barrier.

**Mara (whisper):** *"You're not copying me. You're… listening."*

## Henn's Office — Recording 1 (reel-to-reel)
> **HENN (1961):** *"October the ninth. Today the third patient was linked. I had expected resistance, some distress. Instead, quiet. They looked at each other and… smiled. The one with the burns, Agnes, said, 'I don't hurt as much.' She was crying. I was crying.*
>
> *I have been a physician for thirty years and I have never made anyone's pain smaller. Not once. Tonight, I did. God forgive me, I am happy."*

## Henn's Office — Recording 2 (the last tape)
> **HENN (the night of the flood):** *"The water is at the second step. The staff are gone. I could go too. The door is open. But if I go, they will wake in the dark with nobody feeling them.*
>
> *I have linked myself to the youngest ones so they won't be alone. If you are hearing this, and it is not 1962, then I failed in the only thing that mattered, which was to let them rest.*
>
> *If you can feel this: please be gentle. They have been hurt so very much. They only want to be held."*

**Mara (after, almost to herself):** *"Be gentle."* *(a beat)* *"I was never gentle with myself. How am I supposed to…"*

## Closing Beat
A **service stair** door swings open behind the desk, on its own. The Understudy stands at the top of the stair, waiting.

# WARD 4 — THE MIRROR WARD
*(Full scene in `MIRROR_WARD_SCENE.md`.)*

## Supporting documents (found on the way up)

**Staff memo, March 1962 (taped to a dormitory door):**
> *"The patients are not to be separated, under any circumstances. Last night Sister Agnes was moved to Ward B for her fever. All forty beds woke at once. One of them screamed for an hour. We moved her back."*

**A child's drawing (back of a door):** Two stick figures, one tall, one small, standing under a window with a big sun. Above them: **"WHEN MARA COMES WE WILL GO HOME."**

**Radio, between Wards 4 and 5 — TOMAS:**
> *"You went to the ward. I'm sorry. I'm so sorry. I should have told you from the start. Come down to the boiler house. I'll explain. It's only fair that you hear it from me."*

# WARD 5 — THE BOILER HOUSE

## Arrival
Heat hits like a wall. Orange light moves on soot-black brick. A single firebox glows, burning with no fuel.

**Mara (coughing, to herself):** *"This is… still burning. Thirty years. How?"*

## Meeting Tomas
He stands at the far end of the hall, a stooped man in a grey orderly coat, hands raised, empty. White stubble, careful eyes. He doesn't approach.

> **TOMAS:** *"You have your sister's chin. I wasn't sure it was you, at first. Please… don't scream. I'm not going to hurt you."*
>
> **MARA:** *"You're the voice on the radio."*
>
> **TOMAS:** *"Tomas Ilves. I was an orderly here in sixty-two. I'm… the one who locked the doors."*

## Tomas's Confession (in his room, among the tally marks)
> **TOMAS:** *"The night of the flood, I heard them. Not screaming, humming. All forty-one of them, humming in the dark, one note. The water was rising and the fire had started and Dr. Henn was down there with them, and I thought they were dead already, or worse. So I chained the doors. I told myself it was mercy.*
>
> *I went back three days later, after the fire. They weren't dead. I could feel it through the walls. I couldn't unlock them. I couldn't bring myself to look. I've been here ever since.*
>
> *(He gestures at the tally marks.)*
> *Every day, I make a mark. Eleven thousand, give or take. I feed the boilers because the first winter I let them die, it screamed. All of it. Every nerve in the building. The ground shook. I've kept it warm ever since.*
>
> *Then, in seventy-nine, someone brought a little girl."*
>
> **MARA:** *"Nell."*
>
> **TOMAS:** *"She wasn't afraid of it. She sat in the dark and said hello. And it… answered. For the first time in seventeen years, it answered. She's the only thing that keeps it from screaming."*

**Mara:** *"Then I take her out of there."*

> **TOMAS:** *"If you take her out, it will die. If it dies, the pain goes where? You don't know. I don't know. I've been afraid of the answer for thirty years.*
>
> *She's happy, Mara. In her way. She's loved down there. I just… I can't tell if that's true or if it's what I need to believe."*

## The Collapse
A deep groan from the ceiling. The gantry above the flooded pit gives way, taking part of the floor with it. Tomas is caught on the wrong side, a beam across his leg.

> **TOMAS (calling):** *"Go! Go down! Don't you dare stop for me!"*

**Choice:** Mara can free Tomas (a hard, costly rescue, and he survives) or leave him (the building takes him).

**If rescued:**
> **TOMAS (hoarse):** *"Why?"*
> **MARA:** *"Because someone should have done it for them. Because I'm a surgeon, and I don't leave people on the floor."*

**If left:** His last words, over the radio, fading: *"Tell her… tell her I kept the fire. Tell her I'm sorry."* Then static.

# WARD 6 — THE FOUNDATIONS

## The Descent
Silence. Only water and a heartbeat that isn't Mara's, then a second, then a third.

**Mara (in the vaults, among the faces):** *"Forty-one. Forty-one of them. They're… resting. They're not dead. Oh God."*

## The Chorus Speaks
As Mara walks, the water answers. The voices come as breaths, then whispers, then one chord:

> **THE CHORUS (many voices, soft):** *"Hello."*
> *"You hurt."*
> *"We know."*
> *"We hurt too."*
> *"Stay."*

## Nell — First Words
She opens her eyes slowly. Her voice comes in two layers: her own, clear, and a faint overlap of other voices.

> **NELL:** *"Mara."*
>
> **MARA:** *"Nell."* *(She cannot move.)* *"I… I'm so sorry. I signed. I didn't know, I didn't—"*
>
> **NELL:** *"I know. They told me. They feel everything you feel, you know. Even from far away. Every year, on my birthday, something in the walls hurt a lot. Like grief. I think it was you."*
>
> **MARA:** *"It was."*
>
> **NELL:** *"I was never angry. I want you to know that. Not once."*

## Nell Explains
> **NELL:** *"They're not monsters. They're like a big child who has been hurt for a very long time and doesn't understand why. Tom's still scared of the dark. Agnes still hurts in her chest. They found you because you're the first new hurt in thirty years, and you hurt just like me.*
>
> *They've been trying to take it away. That's what the limp was, the copying. They thought if they felt it too, it wouldn't hurt you so much."*
>
> **MARA:** *"That's… what we do. Surgeons. That's what we—"* *(She stops.)* *"That's what I was trying to do all along."*

## Nell Teaches (so no earlier tape is required)
> **NELL:** *"They only want to be held. Dr. Henn used to say: be gentle. It's the only thing that ever worked."*
> *(She lifts one hand toward the cords above her. Some are frayed, some burned.)*
> **NELL:** *"They're hurt too. If you want to help them, you can start there."*

*(This opens the Bedside Care sequence: Mara may treat the Chorus's own wounds with any remaining supplies. See `TRADEOFFS.md` §8.)*

## Nell Asks
> **NELL:** *"I'm tired, Mara. Not sick. Just tired. I think we all are. I don't know what's right. You're the doctor. What do you want to happen?"*

*(The player now chooses by action, not dialogue; the ending is read from their treatment history. The three scenes follow.)*

# ENDING SCENES

## A. Severance — "Operate"
*(Reached through the surgery.)*

> **MARA:** *"This is going to hurt. I'll be right here."*
> **NELL:** *"It already hurts. It's okay."*

The final cord is cut. The room dims, one light at a time. The faces in the niches smile, close their eyes, and are still.

> **NELL (whisper, her own voice only):** *"Oh. It's quiet. It's… so quiet."*
> **MARA:** *"Breathe. That's it. That's all yours."*

The pain pours into Mara: every old wound, every scar, a chord of ache she will carry. She stands, limping.

**Final scene:** Dawn on the flooded drive. Nell leans on Mara, wrapped in her coat. They walk to the car together at the pace of the slower of them.

> **NELL:** *"You're limping."*
> **MARA:** *"I know."* *(a small smile)* *"It's okay. Somebody has to."*

## B. Vessel — "Stay"
> **MARA:** *"Lie back. I've got them."*
> **NELL:** *"Mara, you can't—"*
> **MARA:** *"I've been carrying this for fifteen years. I'm good at it."*

The cords turn toward her, soft as breath. Nell sits up, gasping, free. The hum settles into a calm, sustained chord.

**Final scene:** Morning light on the sanatorium, now clean and quiet. The flood has receded. In the third-floor window, a figure raises one hand in greeting.

> **NELL (on the drive, to the camera, quietly):** *"She said she'd visit."*

*(The camera holds on the window as the hand lowers.)*

## C. Release — "Let go"
*(Earned only through careful, compassionate treatment throughout.)*

Mara sits beside her sister, takes the cold hand of the Understudy, and does what she learned to do through the whole building: breathes slowly, checks, soothes, holds.

> **MARA (to the Chorus, gently):** *"It's all right. It doesn't have to hurt anymore. You don't have to carry it alone. We'll carry it together, just for a minute. Then you can put it down."*

The cords loosen. A hundred faces breathe out. The glow dims into an ordinary dark.

> **THE CHORUS (very soft, a single voice):** *"Thank you."*

**Final scene:** Mara walks out alone, hands steady, scars faded. Nell, awake and whole, follows a few paces behind, then catches up and takes her hand. The tower clock, for the first time, moves: 2:48.

---

# EPILOGUE (all endings, 15 seconds)
A hand-drawn postcard slides across a table. It reads, in Mara's handwriting, in Nell's hand:

> *"Dear Mara. I'm writing to you the way I always did. I wanted to say: thank you for coming."*

Fade to black. The hum, one note, fades with it.
