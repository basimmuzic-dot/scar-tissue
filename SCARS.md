# SCAR TISSUE — Scar Catalog

*A scar is what's left when a wound is survived. Scars are permanent, small, and never run-ending. Together they make Mara's body a record of how the player played.*

---

## 1. Design Rules

1. **Scars are modifiers, not punishments.** Each has one clear downside and, where possible, a trade-off upside or an adaptation that reduces it.
2. **Readable.** The player can always see where a scar came from and what it does. Scars are shown on the in-world Body Chart.
3. **Capped.** Maximum **3 scars active per zone** and **12 total**. New scars beyond cap *merge* into an existing one (e.g., two "Old Break" scars become "Fused Break"), so the player's body never becomes unplayable.
4. **Adaptable.** Most scars have a **Compensation Technique** that the player can learn through play to reduce the penalty by up to ~50%.
5. **Never block progress.** No scar disables a required interaction. It slows, adds noise, or adds risk.
6. **Shape the ending.** Scar *type mix* (careless, careful, compassionate) feeds the hidden treatment-philosophy tracker (see Story Bible §6).
7. **Accessibility.** Sensory scars (tinnitus, blur, sway) respect the accessibility sliders and have non-sensory fallback cues.

### Scar origin
A wound becomes a scar if it:
- Is **left untreated** past its healing window, or
- Is **treated badly** (a rushed bandage, a poor splint), or
- Is **a severe injury** that heals into one, or
- Is **earned** through a story event (Ward 5 rescue, Ward 6).

Well-treated wounds heal **clean**, with no scar and a small *Care* credit that lowers the entity's agitation.

## 7. Positive / Adaptive Scars (Earned Strengths)

Careful play can produce **callus-like adaptations**, scars with an upside. These reward the compassionate, skilled player and reinforce "harm as something you grow around."

| # | Scar | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 29 | **Surgeon's Callus** | Completing a Hard Surgery challenge | Treatment mini-games gain a wider success window. |
| 30 | **Steady Hands** | Treating 10 wounds cleanly | Reduces tremor from other hand scars by a further 25%. |
| 31 | **Quiet Step** | Learning Weight Shift and Heel-Toe | Footsteps permanently slightly quieter. |
| 32 | **Listener** | Surviving 3 stalking encounters in silence | Entity proximity is signaled by a subtle cue in your own breathing. |

## 8. Interaction Matrix (Examples)
| Combo | Result |
|---|---|
| Old Break + Tinnitus | Limp noise is harder to self-monitor: the player compensates with visual cues (footprint ripples in water). |
| Hearing Loss + Fused Break | Entity can approach from your bad side while your steps are predictable: a high-tension, still fair, combination. |
| Steady Hands + Pale Seam | Tremor mostly cancelled, rewarding careful play. |

## 9. Tuning Notes
- Target average **4–6 scars** in a typical 10-hour run. Fewer than 2 suggests the player is ultra-cautious (fine); more than 10 suggests the injury system is too punishing (retune hazard odds).
- Each scar needs: icon, Body Chart visual, an audio/animation signature, and a short in-world description Mara can voice.
- Playtest every scar in isolation and in pairs to confirm none is un-adaptable and none breaks a required interaction.
