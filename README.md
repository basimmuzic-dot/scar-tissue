# Scar Tissue

Design documents for **Scar Tissue**, a 3D first-person survival horror game in which injuries are physical states with lasting consequences. A wound changes how you move and hear, and the entity hunting you feels your pain.

*Premise:* In 1994, trauma surgeon Mara Voss returns to the flooded St. Aldric Sanatorium to find the sister she once signed away, and learns that what stalks her is not copying her pain but feeling it.

This repository holds design documents only. There is no playable build yet.

## Reading order
1. `GDD.md`: pitch, psychology framing, core loop, injury system
2. `STORY.md`: backstory, characters, six wards, three endings
3. `WORLD_AND_PLOT.md`: the building as the player sees it, and the plot scene by scene
4. `OPENING_CINEMATIC.md`: cold open and title sequence
5. `WARDS_1_2.md`: vertical slice (Admissions and the Baths)
6. `MIRROR_WARD_SCENE.md`: the emotional centre of the game
7. `WARDS_3_6_SCRIPTS.md`: dialogue and endings for the later wards
8. `CAMERA_WALKTHROUGH.md`: shot-by-shot briefs for concept art
9. `FOUND_DOCUMENTS.md`: placement list for documents, tapes and details

## Systems
