## Placement Rules for Level Designers
1. **Critical items are on sightlines of the main route.** A player who never leaves the path should still understand the plot.
2. **Optional items reward looking, not backtracking.** They are in side rooms, behind furniture, or in the light of the player's own flashlight.
3. **One reveal per room.** Never stack two major reveals in a single space.
4. **Everything physical must feel handled.** Dust, wear and placement should show *who* touched it last: Nell (neat, small), Tomas (careful, tidy), Henn (formal), the Chorus (arranged, ritual).
5. **Voice documents are short.** Tapes under 90 seconds; written notes under 60 words, with a pull-quote visible in the first line.
6. **Audio logs are never the only carrier of a critical beat.** A visual or environmental echo must always accompany it.
